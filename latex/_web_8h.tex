Global inline function to determine if two webs interfere. 

Two webs interfere if they share at least one active program point. However, if they intersect at a point where one web exactly ends (last use) and the other exactly begins (definition), they do not interfere at that specific point. {\bfseries{Time Complexity:\+}} O(L\+\_\+1 + L\+\_\+2), where L\+\_\+1 and L\+\_\+2 are the number of active lines in w1 and w2 respectively, driven by {\ttfamily std::\+set\+\_\+intersection}.
